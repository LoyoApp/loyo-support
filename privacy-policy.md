# LOYO – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 11, 2026**

LOYO – Loyalty Cards (“LOYO”, “the app”) is a mobile application that helps users organize and access their loyalty cards.

## Information We Handle

LOYO allows users to add loyalty card information, including store names and barcode numbers. This information is stored locally on the user's device for use within the app.

LOYO does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name or email address to use the app.

## Camera and Photo Access

LOYO may use the device camera to scan loyalty card barcodes. Users may also choose to import a loyalty card image using the device's photo picker.

Camera access is used for barcode scanning. Photo access is initiated by the user when importing an image.

## Data Sharing

LOYO does not sell users' loyalty card information.

## Data Storage and Deletion

Loyalty card information is stored on the user's device. Users can remove cards from within the app. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored app data.

## Children's Privacy

LOYO is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.
